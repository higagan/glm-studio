Date: 2026-06-01

📊 Executive Summary

Jobizo is India's first healthcare HR tech company with 10M+ shifts fulfilled, 150+ hospitals, and 60K+ professionals. However, their job-seeker-first approach and allopathic-only coverage create significant gaps that Medibrick can exploit.

Medibrick's Core Advantage: Institution-first platform covering allopathic + AYUSH + nurses + technicians, with guaranteed 95% show rate and 24h payment.

2. No-Show Problem: ZERO Protection

Industry Problem:
- Staff confirms but doesn't show up
- Better offer elsewhere
- Multiple bookings
- Zero penalty for no-shows
- No verification
- Industry show rate: ~70%

Jobizo's Approach:
- Verified profiles (basic)
- No deposit system
- No backup matching
- No strike system
- No real-time tracking

Medibrick's Solution:

Feature Jobizo Medibrick
Identity Verification Basic Multi-layer
Deposit System ❌ No ✅ ₹500-2000
Calendar Lock ❌ No ✅ Prevents double-booking
Instant Backup ❌ No ✅ #2 auto-promoted
Strike System ❌ No ✅ 3 no-shows = suspended
Real-time Tracking ❌ No ✅ Confirmed → En route → Arrived
Target Show Rate ~70% 95%+

3. Payment Delay: 2-4 Weeks

Jobizo Process:
- Professional completes shift
- Hospital pays platform (whenever)
- Platform processes payment
- Professional receives: 2-4 weeks later
- No transparency into status

Medibrick Process:
- Hospital deposits payment in escrow upfront
- Professional completes shift
- Payment released within 24h
- Dispute window: 48h for hospital
- Full tracking: Pending → Processing → Paid

Professional Pain Point: "I completed my shift but wait 2-4 weeks for payment, chase admin, get frustrated, leave platform."

4. Institution-Centric vs Job-Seeker Centric

Jobizo: Professional searches → applies → waits for response
- Job-seeker first
- Professionals compete for limited spots
- Hospitals get overwhelmed with applications

Medibrick: Hospital posts shift → deposits payment → professionals apply → instant matching
- Institution-first
- Hospital controls the pipeline
- Verified professionals only
- Quality over quantity

💡 Key Insights

  1. Don't compete head-to-head on allopathic doctors. Jobizo owns that.
  2. Own the gaps — Ayurvedic, nursing, technicians, small clinics.
  3. Solve real pain — No-shows and payment delays are universal problems.
  4. Build density first — One city, deep penetration, then expand.
  5. Make Jobizo's strength their weakness — Their size means they can't serve niche segments well.

Bottom Line: Medibrick doesn't need to beat Jobizo at their game. Medibrick needs to play a different game — one Jobizo can't or won't play.
